Privacy Policy

How AmaliticZ handles your data and Amazon-related activity

Effective Date: 06/30/2025 · Last Updated: 2026-10-22

Amazon Affiliate Disclosure
AmaliticZ participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites and apps to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites.

As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide spending analytics functionality:

  • User-imported Amazon purchase history (e.g., product title, ASIN, purchase date, historical purchase price)
  • Basic Amazon account data via Login with Amazon (name, email, and permitted profile fields only)
  • App usage data (feature usage, session duration, performance metrics)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide personal spending analytics and insights
  • Analyze purchase patterns and historical spending behavior
  • Allow optional navigation to Amazon product pages when you choose
  • Improve app performance, stability, and user experience

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Amazon Affiliate Links

Some links in AmaliticZ may be Amazon affiliate links. If you click an Amazon link and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Affiliate links are generated only when you explicitly choose to open a product on Amazon. No automatic redirection or background link generation occurs.

4. Login with Amazon (LWA)

We use Login with Amazon (LWA) solely for authentication purposes. We do not access or store your Amazon password. We only receive limited account information authorized by Amazon's OAuth scope.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your imported Amazon purchase history is stored securely and used only to provide analytics within the app. You may delete your data at any time.

6. How Long We Keep Data

Account information and imported order history are kept while your account is active, and deleted when you ask us to delete them.

To ask for account or data deletion, email privacy@amaliticz.com.

7. Third-Party Services

We use these service providers:

  • Amazon — Login with Amazon and the Amazon Associates program
  • Supabase — accounts and password reset
  • FormSubmit — the support form on this website

We also use trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and analytics services) under strict confidentiality obligations.

8. Amazon Associates

Affiliate links in AmaliticZ follow the Amazon Associates program.

9. Your Rights

  • Request deletion of your stored data
  • Disconnect Login with Amazon access
  • Request information about stored data

10. What This App Is / Is Not

AmaliticZ is a personal spending analytics tool.

  • It is not affiliated with or operated by Amazon
  • It is not an Amazon shopping app
  • It does not sell or fulfill products
  • It does not provide real-time Amazon pricing or inventory
